ENGLISH STORY

Year 2142. The world became a drawing. Project LINE tried to turn humans into pure lines. It failed.

Humanity became Stick Beings - creatures drawn with a single stroke. Memory erased. Only instinct remains: fight to survive.

You are REX-07, the last with memory. The only hope is the Core Pen - shattered into 3 fragments across the realm. Find them. Survive. Face the Ink Lord who drew this hell.
